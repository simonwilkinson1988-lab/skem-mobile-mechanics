Common questions

Stuff people always ask us.

Do you charge a call-out fee?
No. Never have, never will. You only pay for the work we do and the parts we use. If we quote you and you decide not to go ahead, you owe us nothing.
How quickly can you come out?
Most jobs we can book within 24-48 hours. For urgent stuff — breakdowns, battery failures, MOT repairs needed ASAP — we can often do same day. Just ring us and we'll see what we can do.
What areas do you cover?
Skelmersdale, Up Holland, Ormskirk, Rainford, Burscough, Parbold, Appley Bridge, Wigan, St Helens — roughly a 15-mile radius. If you're not sure, just ask. See full area list →
Do you supply parts?
Yes. We get parts at trade prices from trusted suppliers and pass the savings onto you. No retail markup. All parts meet or exceed manufacturer specifications. If you've already got your own parts, we're happy to fit those too.
What vehicles do you work on?
Private cars and light commercial vans — all makes and models. Petrol, diesel, hybrid. If you've got something unusual, just ask and we'll let you know.
Can you do it at my workplace?
Absolutely. As long as there's space to work safely and your employer doesn't mind, we're happy to come to your work car park. Loads of our customers do this — car gets fixed while you're at your desk.
Do you stamp the service book?
Yes. Every service gets a stamp in your book so your warranty and resale value stay intact. Same quality parts, same standards — just without the garage overheads.
What if it rains?
We work in all weather. Got a gazebo for the worst days. Unless it's absolutely lashing sideways and would compromise the quality of the work, we crack on regardless. We're not made of sugar.
