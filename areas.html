Local knowledge

We're not some national franchise parachuting in. We live here, we work here, we know the area.

We know the roads

The potholes on Grimshaw Road that smash springs. The speed bumps on Birch Green that kill drop links. The rat runs that chew through brake pads. We see the same jobs from the same areas — and we stock the parts accordingly.

We know the people

Most of our work comes through word of mouth. Someone's mate tells their mate, who tells their nan. That's how we like it. Real recommendations from real people round here.

We know what garages charge

We know what the local garages are quoting for the same work. And we know we can do it for less, at your door, without the wait. If you've got a quote from somewhere else — ring us and we'll see if we can beat it.
