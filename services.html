What we do

Professional repairs and servicing — done at your door. No garage trip needed. We bring the tools, the parts, and the know-how to you.

We work on all makes and models — private cars up to light commercial vans. Quality parts from trusted suppliers at trade prices. If you're not sure whether we can help, just ask.

Brakes FROM £80

Your brakes are the most important safety system on your motor. Whether it's squealing, grinding, a spongy pedal, or a warning light — don't ignore it. We'll sort it on your drive, usually within the hour.

  • Brake pad replacement (front or rear)
  • Brake disc and pad combos
  • Caliper repair and replacement
  • Brake fluid change and bleeding
  • Brake pipe repair and replacement
  • Handbrake cable adjustment
  • Drum brakes and shoes
  • Full brake inspections

Full & Interim Servicing FROM £99

Regular servicing keeps your car sweet and stops small problems turning into expensive ones. We do the lot at your door — and we stamp the service book so your warranty stays intact.

  • Interim service — oil & filter, basic checks
  • Full service — comprehensive inspection, all fluids, all filters
  • Major service — full service plus spark plugs, fuel filter, the lot
  • All work to manufacturer standards
  • Quality oils and OE-spec filters
  • Service book stamped on completion

MOT Failure Repairs

Failed your MOT? Don't let the testing station rip you off on the repair. Ring us, tell us what's on the fail sheet, and we'll come to you and sort it for less. Then you drive straight back for the retest.

  • Brake pads, discs and pipes
  • Suspension arms, bushes and drop links
  • Bulbs and lighting faults
  • Emissions and exhaust issues
  • Tyre replacements
  • Wipers and washers
  • Steering components
  • Corroded or damaged bodywork advisories

Diagnostics FROM £40

Got a warning light staring at you? Don't just get a code read and guesswork from Halfords. We use dealer-level diagnostic equipment to actually find out what's wrong — not just what the code says.

  • Engine management light investigation
  • Fault code reading AND interpretation
  • Live data analysis
  • ABS and traction control diagnostics
  • Airbag warning light diagnostics
  • Sensor testing and replacement
  • Pre-purchase vehicle inspections
  • Electrical fault finding

Battery Services

Won't start? Clicking? Sluggish? It's probably the battery. We'll test it, and if it's dead, we'll fit a new one there and then. Most of the time we're with you within the hour.

  • Battery health testing
  • Battery supply and fitting (same day)
  • Emergency battery call-outs
  • Charging system checks
  • Alternator testing and replacement
  • Jump starts

Suspension & Steering

Knocking over bumps? Pulling to one side? Tyres wearing unevenly? That's suspension or steering on its way out. We'll get underneath, find the culprit, and replace it at your door.

  • Coil springs (snapped or sagging)
  • Shock absorbers (dampers)
  • Anti-roll bar drop links
  • Track rod ends and rack boots
  • Wheel bearings
  • Wishbones and suspension arms
  • Ball joints
  • Top mounts and bushes

Clutch Repairs

Slipping, juddering, biting at the top, or not engaging at all? Clutch work isn't cheap — but we're a damn sight cheaper than any garage. And we do it properly, not just slap a friction plate on and hope for the best.

  • Complete clutch kit replacement
  • Dual mass flywheel (DMF) replacement
  • Concentric slave cylinder (CSC)
  • Master cylinder replacement
  • Clutch cable replacement
  • Clutch fluid bleeding
  • Flywheel inspection and resurfacing

Exhausts & Cooling

Blowing exhaust? Overheating? Coolant light on? These things only get worse if you leave them. We'll find the leak, patch it or replace it, and get you back on the road safe.

  • Exhaust repair and section replacement
  • Catalytic converter replacement
  • Radiator replacement
  • Thermostat replacement
  • Water pump replacement
  • Coolant hoses and pipes
  • Head gasket diagnosis
  • Heater matrix issues

General Repairs & Maintenance

Not everything fits neatly into a category. If your car's doing something weird, making a noise, leaking, or just needs a bit of TLC — ring us. We probably do it.

  • Timing belt and chain replacement
  • Alternator and starter motor
  • Engine mounts
  • Drive belts (aux/serpentine)
  • Spark plug replacement
  • Bulbs, wipers, and small jobs
  • Pre-MOT checks and advisories
  • Oil changes and top-ups

Not sure what you need?

Just tell us the symptoms. We'll figure out what's wrong and give you a straight price before we start anything.
