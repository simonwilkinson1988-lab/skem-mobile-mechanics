Our story

Why we started Skem Mobile Mechanics.

We've been fixing cars for over 20 years. Started as an apprentice, spent years in main dealers, then independent garages. Saw the same thing everywhere — customers getting overcharged, waiting days for their car back, and being talked down to when they asked a question.

Got sick of it. So we went mobile.

The idea was simple: turn up at people's houses, fix their car on the drive, charge a fair price, and treat them like adults. No waiting rooms. No courtesy car hassle. No phoning three days later saying "we've found more wrong with it" after the car's already in bits.

We pass our trade prices on parts straight to you. We don't have the overheads of a big unit with a forecourt and three receptionists. That means you pay less for the same quality work.

We're not trying to build some empire. We just want to do good work, keep people on the road, and not rip anyone off in the process. That's it.
